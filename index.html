Open Shelf: Open Source Font Library

Every typeface in the public library is free and open source, mostly under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so you can use, embed and modify it in personal and commercial work. Check the licence file that ships with each font before you rely on it.

Font details and pairing provenance were compiled from public sources and widely shared recommendations. They are a starting point, not an authority. About Open Shelf and font licences
